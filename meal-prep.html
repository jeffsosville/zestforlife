How it works

Who it's for

Busy weeks, health goals, and everyone in between.

Busy families who want real dinners without the nightly scramble.

Special diets — gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, lower-sodium and more.

Senior meal delivery

Meals for older adults, delivered with care.

Many of our clients are older adults living at home — and their grown children, who arrange meals from nearby or far away. Meals are easy to reheat, clearly labeled, and delivered on the same day every week.

Questions

Good to know.

How much does meal prep cost?

It depends on how many meals per week and any custom requirements. Send a quick note or text and we'll put together a plan and price.

Which days do you deliver?

Each town has a set delivery day so routes stay short and meals arrive fresh. We'll confirm yours when you sign up.

Can I set up meals for my parent?

Yes. Many families arrange weekly meals for a parent or grandparent. We'll work with you on the menu, any diet guidance from their doctor, and delivery.

How long do meals keep?

Meals are labeled with a use-by date and reheating instructions. Most keep 3–4 days in the refrigerator, and many dishes freeze well.

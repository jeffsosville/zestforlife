Questions

Good to know.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in the Village of Skaneateles and serve Skaneateles, Skaneateles Falls, Mottville, Marcellus, Elbridge, Owasco and Auburn — roughly a five-mile radius of the village plus Marcellus and Auburn. For catering we can often travel farther.

Do you cook for special diets?

Yes. Every plan is built around the people eating it: gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, lower-sodium, heart-healthy, softer textures and smaller portions are all part of the everyday work. If a doctor or dietitian has given guidance, send it along and meals will follow it.

Do you cook meals for older adults?

Yes — it's one of the things we do most. Families often set up weekly meals for a parent or grandparent: easy-to-reheat portions, clearly labeled, delivered on a regular day, with menus that respect any diet the doctor has recommended.

How does weekly meal prep work?

Choose from the weekly menu (or ask for a custom plan), place your order by the cutoff, and meals are delivered on your town's delivery day, portioned and labeled with reheating instructions.

How far ahead should I book catering?

Two to four weeks is ideal for parties and gatherings, and longer for large events or summer weekends on the lake. Last-minute? Text anyway — if there's room on the calendar we'll make it work.

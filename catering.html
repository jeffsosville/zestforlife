How it works

Drop-off, or full service with a bar.

Drop-off catering: platters and buffet trays delivered ready to serve, labeled, with simple setup.

Full-service catering: on-site cooking, plating and service, with cleanup afterward.

Bartending: add bar service and a signature cocktail to any event.

Service area

Skaneateles, Marcellus, Auburn — and everywhere in between.

Zest for Life is based in the Village of Skaneateles and serves homes and events within about five miles of the village, plus Marcellus and Auburn. Weekly meal-prep deliveries run on a set day for each town, so routes stay short and food arrives fresh.

Questions

Good to know.

How far ahead should I book catering?

Two to four weeks is ideal, and longer for large events or summer weekends. Last-minute? Text anyway — if the calendar has room we'll make it work.

Can you handle allergies and special diets?

Yes. Gluten-free, dairy-free, vegetarian, vegan and allergy-aware dishes are planned into the menu from the start, and labeled at the event.

Do you provide bartending?

Yes — bar service and a signature cocktail can be added to any catered event.

What areas do you cater?

Skaneateles and towns within about five miles, plus Marcellus and Auburn. For larger events we often travel farther — just ask.

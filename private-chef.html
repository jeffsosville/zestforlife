What's included

You enjoy the evening. We handle the rest.

Private chef service is personal by design. We start with a short conversation about the occasion, your guests, and how you like to eat, then put together a menu just for you.

  • Custom menu planning around your tastes and diets
  • Grocery shopping, sourced local wherever possible
  • Cooking and plating in your kitchen
  • Service at the table, if you'd like it
  • Full kitchen cleanup before we leave

Questions

Good to know.

What's the difference between a private chef and catering?

A private chef cooks in your home for a smaller group, with the menu built around you. Catering is food for an event, delivered or served on site.

Do you shop for groceries?

Yes. Grocery planning and shopping is included, with local ingredients wherever we can find them.

Can you cook for a parent who lives alone?

Yes. A weekly in-home visit to cook and portion meals is a popular option for older adults, and menus follow any guidance from their doctor or dietitian.

Where do you work?

In homes across Skaneateles and towns within about five miles, plus Marcellus and Auburn.
